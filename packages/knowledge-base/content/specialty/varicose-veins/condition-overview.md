Varicose veins are enlarged, twisted veins that most often appear in the legs.
They develop when the small valves inside a vein that normally keep blood
flowing back toward the heart weaken, so some blood pools in the vein.

Common signs a patient may describe:

- Rope-like or bulging veins that are visible under the skin
- Aching, heaviness, or tiredness in the legs, often worse at the end of the day
- Swelling around the ankles
- Itching or restless legs

Many people have varicose veins. For some they are mainly a cosmetic concern;
for others they cause discomfort or point to an underlying vein condition worth
evaluating. A consultation is how the practice determines which is the case.
