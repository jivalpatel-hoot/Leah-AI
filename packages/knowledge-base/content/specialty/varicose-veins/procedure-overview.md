Modern vein treatment is usually done in the office and does not require the
older "vein stripping" surgery. The specific approach is decided by the
physician after evaluating the patient, commonly with an ultrasound.

A typical treatment path the practice may discuss at consultation:

1. **Consultation and ultrasound** — the physician maps the veins and confirms
   whether treatment is appropriate.
2. **In-office procedure** — common options include closing the affected vein
   with heat (thermal ablation) or a medical adhesive, or treating smaller veins
   with injections (sclerotherapy). Each is minimally invasive.
3. **Recovery** — most patients walk out the same day and return to normal
   activity quickly. The physician gives specific aftercare instructions.
