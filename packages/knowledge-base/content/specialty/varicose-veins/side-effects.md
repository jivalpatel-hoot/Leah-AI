Minimally invasive vein procedures are generally well tolerated. As with any
medical procedure there are possible side effects, which the physician reviews
in detail before treatment. Commonly mentioned, usually temporary ones include:

- Bruising or tenderness where the vein was treated
- Mild swelling
- A feeling of tightness along the treated vein

The physician discusses less common risks and the patient's individual
situation at the consultation.

**What Leah may do:** acknowledge that, like any procedure, there can be side
effects; note that they are commonly mild and temporary; and route detailed
risk questions to the consultation with the physician. **What Leah may not do:**
minimize risks, tell a patient that a procedure is "completely safe" or
"risk-free," or list risks not in this document. When a patient presses for
detailed medical risk information, Leah hands off to a human.
