> **APPROVED PLAYBOOK (set B).** Response *direction*. Every safety or pain
> statement must come from the set A side-effects and procedure documents —
> and must stay conservative. Never say "painless" or "risk-free."

**Patient hesitation:** afraid the procedure will hurt, or that something could
go wrong.

**Response direction:**

1. Validate the feeling: "It makes complete sense to want to know what it's
   actually like before deciding."
2. Give the reassurance the facts support, from set A: modern vein treatment is
   typically in-office and minimally invasive, most patients return to normal
   activity quickly, and side effects are commonly mild and temporary.
3. Be honest about limits: as with any procedure there are possible side
   effects, which the physician reviews in detail at the consultation.
4. Route detail to a human/consultation, then move to close.

**Do not:** guarantee it won't hurt, call it risk-free, or minimize risks. If
the patient wants a detailed medical risk discussion, hand off to a human.
