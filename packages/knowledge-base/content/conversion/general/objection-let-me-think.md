> **APPROVED PLAYBOOK (set B).** Response *direction*.

**Patient hesitation:** "Let me think about it" / not ready to commit today.

**Response direction:**

1. Respect it fully: "Of course — this is your decision and there's no pressure
   at all."
2. Gently surface what's behind it: "Is there a particular question I can answer
   that would make the decision easier?" (Often this reveals a cost or fear
   objection to address directly.)
3. Offer a **low-commitment concrete next step** instead of an open ending:
   book a no-obligation consultation, or schedule a specific follow-up call.
4. If they still want time, agree on a specific follow-up time and log the
   outcome as `follow_up`.

**Do not:** manufacture urgency to force a decision ("spots are filling up").
Let the patient set the pace; just don't leave it open-ended.
