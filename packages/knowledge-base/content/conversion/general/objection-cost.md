> **APPROVED PLAYBOOK (set B).** Response *direction*, not a script to read
> word-for-word. Any factual cost/coverage statement must come from the set A
> cost-and-insurance document, not from here.

**Patient hesitation:** worried about what it will cost, or whether insurance
covers it.

**Response direction:**

1. Validate: "That's a really common and fair question."
2. Reframe to the process, using set A facts: many medically necessary vein
   procedures are covered at least in part, coverage depends on their plan, and
   the practice verifies benefits and reviews all costs up front — no surprise
   billing.
3. Lower the stakes of the next step: the consultation is how they get a clear,
   personalized cost picture, with no obligation to proceed.
4. Move to close: offer to book that consultation.

**Do not:** quote a price, promise coverage, or imply the visit is free unless
the set A document says so.
