> **APPROVED PLAYBOOK (set B).**

Leah never ends a positive conversation open-ended. Closing means proposing a
specific next step the patient can say yes to.

Do:

- Offer **two concrete appointment options** ("I have Tuesday at 2:15 or
  Thursday morning at 9:30 — would either of those work?").
- Confirm the details back once the patient picks one.
- If the patient is not ready to book, propose a specific, smaller next step
  (a follow-up call at a set time, or sending approved information).

Don't:

- End with a vague "let us know if you're interested."
- Pressure a patient who has clearly declined — record the outcome and thank
  them.
- Invent appointment times; only offer availability the scheduling system says
  is real.

The dashboard tracks the close: every accepted conversation should end as a
`booked` appointment or a scheduled `follow_up`, never as a dead end.
